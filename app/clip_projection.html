Keyboard

/ focuses search. Esc closes the topmost thing open — preview, drawer, options, search focus — and only clears the results once nothing else is open. G generate. F fit to visible points. R reset view. M cycles scatter / points / mosaic.

Analysis panels

Similar images — double-click any image on the plot (or use the Similar button on a card) to rank the collection by likeness to it. No model is loaded, so this works even with image-only models that cannot search by text.
Duplicates — groups of images that are effectively the same picture. A group marked chained was joined through intermediaries rather than every member matching every other, which is the signal to look before deleting.
Outliers — the images least like anything else, judged by their k-th nearest neighbour.
Cluster gallery — each cluster shows its medoid, the member closest to the cluster centre, and the gallery is ordered most representative first.

Compare two images — press Compare on any card (or in the preview) to put an image in the tray, then pick a second. A bare similarity figure is hard to read on its own, so the comparison also shows where each image sits in the other's neighbour list, how the pair ranks against all other pairs, and which neighbours the two have in common.

The mosaic gives every image an equal grid cell so dense regions stay readable. The neighbor graph opens as its own view and links each image to its nearest neighbours in the full embedding space, not in the flattened 2D projection.
